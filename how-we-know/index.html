How we know who owns a company

We only label a company when we can link to evidence. Every company page shows its sources and a “How we know” line.

StatusWhat it takes
PE-ownedA press release, news article or filing showing a private equity–backed platform bought it.
Corporate-owned Investor-owned Investor-backedA public source naming the corporate parent or investor.
FranchiseThe company is a franchise location. We name the brand and its owner.
Likely independent*Self-described family/locally owned; not yet owner-verified. The company site or BBB profile names a family or individual owner and our search found no acquisition.
Verified independentOwner-confirmed through an Ohio license check, Ohio Secretary of State filing and owner interview. No company has this status yet.
UnknownNot enough public evidence yet.

Sources we use

  • Acquirer press releases and trade press (ACHR News, Contracting Business)
  • Company About pages and BBB business profiles, including “Date of New Ownership” and alternate names
  • Ohio Secretary of State business records and OCILB license lookups (planned for every entry before launch)

Limits

Buyers often keep the old name and the “family-owned” wording, so a self-description is weak evidence. One example: Fire & Ice is still called locally owned on local lists, but its BBB profile shows new ownership in June 2025 under a Bassett/Ohio Service Partners name. That is why “likely” is never the same as verified. Report an error.
